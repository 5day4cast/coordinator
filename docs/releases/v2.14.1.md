# Coordinator v2.14.1

This release takes the fixes from the first day of v2.14.0 in production, the tester's first round of feedback on the player pages, a per-player ledger, and an operator write-off for escrow refunds that can never finish.

**Arkade** ([#148](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/148), [#149](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/149))

- ark-client is patched (`[patch.crates-io]` → a fork at the fix commit, until upstream releases it): it subscribes to boarding outpoints, ignores events of other batches, and errors instead of submitting an unsigned commitment transaction. A boarding whose batch fails now fails fast with the batch's reason.
- ark-swapd boards in its own task with a timeout, so a boarding no longer holds the wallet against the API and the other worker steps; reporting a paid refund returns 202 and the worker claims.
- The coordinator passes ark-swapd's 503 through: "Lightning payments are unavailable for a moment".

**Tickets** ([#151](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/151), [#161](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/161))

- A retried ticket request for the same entry gets the same ticket, invoice and policy back instead of a 500; a request with a different entry gets a 409 with a plain message.
- The entry form keeps its entry id and key until the entry is paid, so the entry id is the request's idempotency key and a retry after a lost response needs no extra click.

**Escrow refunds** ([#155](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/155), [#160](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/160))

- Cancelled and failed competitions with escrowed fees show the refund line.
- Operators can write off a refund that can never finish (a paid ticket whose player never registered): `coordinator admin write-off-refund (--ticket | --competition) --reason …`, and `POST /api/v1/admin/refunds/write-off`. A written-off refund leaves the cleanup pass, its log line and the refund counts; the reason stays in the operator API and `admin competitions show`. See `docs/ops/stuck-escrow-check.md`.

**Player pages** ([#153](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/153), [#154](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/154), [#159](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/159), [#162](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/162))

- One all-in **Entry fee** everywhere; the breakdown lives in a tooltip on the entry form and the entries page only. The payment dialog reads "Pay N sats by Lightning to enter this competition."
- The picks dialog fits the screen and has a header row (Reading · Pick · Observed · Points); tooltips are readable in the dark theme and stay on a phone's screen; window ends carry the zone ("… 10:02 PM EDT"); the Picks button is 44 px on phones; the entry-form map pins are amber, not blue.
- Paying with no picks says "Make at least one pick"; closing the payment dialog hands the Pay button back and reopening shows the same invoice; every failed request gives the button back with a plain message.
- **Entries ledger:** the signed-in entries page shows what each entry cost and what came back (payout, refund, or pending), with a summary: paid, received (won, refunded) and net, and pending amounts called out separately. Rows page 25 at a time.
- The home intro reads one sentence per line on wide screens; expired competitions say when the oracle could not verify the data.

**Help page** ([#152](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/152), [#156](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/156), [#157](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/157), [#163](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/163))

- The how-it-works video is re-recorded with teal callouts, "Local Forecast" by Kevin MacLeod (CC BY 4.0) under it, and a Safari-safe encoding; desktop viewports get a desktop recording, phones the phone one, each loading only when played.
- A worked example of where an entry fee goes (3% service fee), and no more notes on older competition formats.

**Synth** ([#150](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/150))

- Run pages fit phones; "passed · still live" until the money settles; runs synth stopped following are badged unverified and counted in Stuck money, as are competitions that didn't run with unrefunded escrows.

**Required upgrade steps**

Deploy the coordinator, ark-swapd and synth from `v2.14.1`. The coordinator runs one migration (`ticket_ark_refund_write_offs`) at startup. The verifier enclave is unchanged from v2.14.0.

**Compatibility**

Rolling back to v2.14.0 is safe; the write-off table is ignored by it.

**Validation**

The workspace tests and E2E suite pass in CI.
