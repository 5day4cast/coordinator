# Coordinator and Synth v2.24.0

## Players

A winner whose Lightning payout fails or is delayed by an outage keeps getting Lightning attempts for as long as they could not yet claim on chain. A winner still unpaid after that is owed: the coordinator no longer takes their share on its own, and an operator pays them.
The sign-up dialog says exactly what recovering your funds needs for this deployment: your recovery key, the recovery file from your Payouts page, and, where records are published, our Nostr relay.

## Recovery tool

- `coordinator-recover claim` waits until the split transaction can confirm (`delta` blocks after the outcome) instead of failing, says from which block, and about when, each next step opens, and by when it must confirm.
- One entry's failure no longer stops the others.
- The CLI can fee-bump outcome, expiry and split transactions of contracts with anchors from a coin of the player's own (`--fee-utxo`, key in `COORDINATOR_RECOVER_FEE_KEY`), and unroll Arkade escrows the same way. `--dry-run` prints the children.
- Releases can carry the coordinator's recovery keys and relays (`crates/coordinator-recover/recovery-defaults.json`). The network is taken from the player's records when `--network` is not given.

## Operators

- Owed winners: `coordinator admin owed-winners list|approve-sweep|settle`, the operator page's competition view, and `docs/ops/owed-winners.md`. Alert on `coordinator_winners_owed > 0`.
- Settlement fee-bumps its own stuck anchored outcome, expiry and split transactions from the LND wallet (`[cpfp_settings]`, on by default; see docs/RECOVERY.md). Keep a confirmed on-chain coin in the LND wallet of about (parent + 200 vB) × the fee estimate + 1,000 sats, and alert on `coordinator_cpfp_bumps_total{result=~"no_coin|parent_rejected|failed"}`. Spending P2A anchors needs Bitcoin Core 28 or newer behind LND.
- Funding checks that cannot read the chain are retried with backoff (`coordinator_funding_checks_failing`), and pools a batch funded are resumed if an earlier version failed them.
- The oracle client times out each request after 60 s. Oracle steps are retried for an hour instead of failing at the first error.
- A contract whose expiry transaction confirmed in place of its outcome transaction settles on the expiry outcome.

## Deploying

Deploy Coordinator. Migration `owed_winners` (a new table and the `entries.split_output_spent_at` column) is additive and roll-forward only. No configuration change is required, and no changes to Synth, ark-swapd or the Keymeld gateway and verifier.
