# v2.27.28 — A larger shared Ark indexer budget

v2.27.25 shared ten `GetVtxos` requests per four minutes between ark-swapd, the coordinator and Synth: six for payments and four for background reads. A 20-seat competition needs more than that while its players enter. Entry payments, escrow lookups and wallet readings were refused, ticket requests answered that Lightning payments were unavailable, and competitions that needed every seat filled were cancelled unfilled.

The budget is now 30 requests per four minutes, about 7.5 a minute across all three services: 20 for payments and 10 for background reads. The window, priorities, persistence and per-process pacing are unchanged.
