# v2.27.22 — Synth's last call for empty seats

v2.27.21 fills other people's competitions from 30 minutes before their entries close. One of those players can still fail to pay, and Synth's own competitions were never filled. Either way a seat stays empty and the competition starts short or is cancelled.

`scheduler.fill` now also makes a last call 5 minutes before entries close (`last_call_before_close_secs`, default 300; 0 turns it off). By then every earlier run's players have asked for their seats, so an empty seat is one nobody holds. The last call fills those seats in any competition meant to play, Synth's own included. Its players enter at once: they arrive within 30 seconds, pay within 10 and submit within 20. An entry takes about ten seconds of work besides those waits.

- **Once per competition.** A competition gets one last call.
- **Test runs left alone.** No last call goes to a competition with a Synth run that tests refunds, abandoned seats or cancellation (`escrow_refund`, `queued_too_few`, `queued_leftover_refund`, `abandoned_unpaid`, `paid_abandonment`, `duplicate_submission`, `late_submission`, `stress_full_pool`).
- **Separate players.** A player enters a competition once, so a last call draws from player 100 onward, past any earlier run's.
- **Held seats.** A seat held by an unpaid ticket stays held until its reservation expires.

Synth now tracks its runs in progress by run, not by competition, so a competition's own run and the runs that fill it each show on the dashboard.

Only Synth changes. No migration or configuration change is required: an existing `scheduler.fill` gets the last call by default.
