# Coordinator v2.14.7

This release makes the coordinator, ark-swapd and synth ride out an outage of the Arkade server instead of failing competitions, wasting on-chain top-ups and selling tickets that cannot kick off.

**Why**

Twice on one day the public Arkade server could not build batches: first its fee estimation failed for twelve minutes, then boarding rescans failed for hours. A competition was failed after six kickoff errors in four minutes, synth kept sending on-chain top-ups into a boarding address the server could not board, and tickets were still sold into escrows that could not kick off.

**Coordinator** ([#180](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/180), the pause PR)

- A kickoff whose batch fails on the server is tried again on its usual cadence until the competition's own deadline (the fee-wait after its start, or the signing timeout), and only then fails it, with the server's message in the reason. Each attempt still counts in `coordinator_competition_step_failures_total`.
- While the last server-side batch failure is newer than the last success and under `arkade_outage_secs` (default 900) old, entries are paused: the price quote is disabled, the entry form and the ticket route say "Entries are paused while the Arkade network recovers; try again in a little while", and `coordinator_arkade_unavailable` is 1. The next successful batch step lifts it, and so does silence for the whole window.

**ark-swapd** ([#180](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/180))

- `GET /v1/wallet` gains `boarding_sat`: confirmed coins at the boarding address that the next board would move in. The startup line says how much awaits boarding.

**Synth** ([#180](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/180))

- No on-chain top-up while at least `low_sats` already await boarding; the dashboard's Liquidity line shows the waiting amount.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy ark-swapd, then the coordinator and synth, from `v2.14.7`. No migrations. New optional coordinator setting `arkade_outage_secs`.

**Compatibility**

Rolling back to v2.14.6 is safe.
