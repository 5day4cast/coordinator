# Coordinator v2.14.6

This release carries the fixes from the fourth QA round: honest refund wording, a help page whose links open what they point at, larger tap targets, and fewer wasted invoices when an escrow recovery keeps failing.

**Player UI** ([#177](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/177), [#178](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/178))

- Links into "How the tech works" on the help page render it unfolded and land on "Where your sats go", instead of scrolling to a closed heading.
- A competition whose refunds ended with some written off reads "Not all refunded", and its page says so and asks the player to get in touch; the entry count and the paid-fee count come from the same source. A cancelled competition with paid entries shows its refund line. The pot-return note no longer prints a second price.
- The entry form's price errors speak of the entry fee, never a network fee.
- 44 px tap targets for the entry form's `?` buttons and fee summary, the leaderboard's `?` and Copy.

**Refunds** ([#178](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/178))

- After a batch recovery fails on the Arkade server, the next remint of the ticket's refund waits 6, then 12, then 24 hours instead of a fixed 6, so a server outage costs fewer swap invoices. A migration adds the counter.

**Synth** ([#178](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/178))

- Written-off refunds are no longer listed as stuck money, and a past expiry is never the nearest.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the coordinator and synth from `v2.14.6`. The migration runs on start.

**Compatibility**

Rolling back to v2.14.5 leaves the new column unused.
