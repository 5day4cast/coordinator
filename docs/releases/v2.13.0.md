# Coordinator v2.13.0

This release clears Arkade batch intents that a failed pool kickoff leaves queued, so the escrows they hold can be refunded ([#137](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/137)).

**The problem**

A pool kickoff registers one Arkade batch intent spending every escrow of the pool. If the kickoff failed before its forfeits (a timeout, the event stream ending, a failed confirmation, or a restart), nothing deleted the intent. arkd keeps unconfirmed intents queued with no expiry, so:
- every retry of the kickoff failed with a duplicated input;
- every refund of those escrows failed with `VTXO_ALREADY_REGISTERED`;
- the refund minted a new swap, and asked the player's Lightning Address for a new invoice, every hour.

**Changes**

- **Transport:** `ArkTransport::delete_intent` sends arkd's `DeleteIntent` with a BIP-322 proof over any input of the intent. `VTXO_ALREADY_REGISTERED` and "no matching intents" are recognised by their gRPC status and arkd error code.
- **Kickoff:** before registering, a kickoff deletes any intent a previous attempt left over the pool's escrows. On any failure before its forfeits, it deletes its own intent. A failed delete is only logged, so a kickoff is never blocked by it.
- **Refunds:** when a refund finds its escrow held by a queued intent, it deletes that intent with a proof over this one escrow, then resubmits the transaction it already signed, once. Deleting the intent frees the pool's other escrows too.
  - While a delete keeps failing, the refund is marked as held. It is neither reminted nor signed again, and the problem is reported once.
- **Keymeld verifier:** a new signing purpose, `DeleteIntent`, signs only a proof that moves no value: its only output is an empty `OP_RETURN`, its message is exactly `{"type":"delete","expire_at":N}` with a short expiry, and it commits to that message. The purpose is available under the bound escrow permission (kickoff) and under the existing refund permission (refunds). Existing purposes, their encodings and existing player consents are unchanged.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the coordinator, ark-swapd and synth from `v2.13.0`. There are no migrations or configuration changes.
2. Deploy the verifier enclave from `v2.13.0` with Keymeld (its `verifierVersion` and the enclave tarball's hash). Delete proofs need the new purpose. Until then, the kickoff's deletes fail harmlessly and held refunds wait instead of reminting.

**Compatibility**

Rolling back to v2.12.0 is safe. Refunds marked as held go back to the hourly remint loop.

**Validation**

- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -D warnings` is clean, and the workspace tests and E2E suite pass in CI.
- New tests in the Arkade harnesses cover:
  - a leaked kickoff intent blocking refunds until the first refund deletes it;
  - no remint or re-signing while a delete keeps failing;
  - kickoff cleanup before and after a failure;
  - the verifier refusing delete proofs that pay anything, prove a registration, or name another message.
- It has not yet run against a live arkd, and the delete proof's acceptance by arkd is inferred from its registration path, which uses the same leaf.
