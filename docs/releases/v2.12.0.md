# Coordinator v2.12.0

This release settles contracts that expire without an attestation, fixes refund-swap claims, and acts on the second round of player feedback.

**Settlement and refunds**

- **Expired contracts settle on their expiry outcome** ([#131](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/131)). Before, when the oracle never attested, the coordinator broadcast the expiry transaction and then dropped the competition: no player was refunded and the output stayed on-chain.
  - The expiry transaction is now the contract's outcome transaction for `Outcome::Expiry`. The competition goes through the usual outcome, delta and completed steps. Players are paid their refund shares over Lightning while the payout window is open, and the output is split and reclaimed like any outcome's.
  - Competitions left in `expiry_broadcasted` by earlier versions are moved on the same way. Their payout windows have passed, so their outputs are reclaimed.
  - The escrow protocol's payout request carries an optional attestation. The verifier and the browser wallet settle a request without one on the expiry outcome, and only once the contract has expired. Attested requests serialize exactly as before.
- **Refund swaps are claimed** ([#134](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/134)). ark-swapd's claim witness left an empty stack under the claim leaf's `EQUALVERIFY VERIFY`, and arkd reported that as `INVALID_SIGNATURE`, so no refund swap had ever been claimed. The witness is now `[0x01, preimage]`; swap scripts and addresses are unchanged. Refunds that passed their deadline after paying the player are claimed where the player has not taken the swap back.
- **Escrow refunds wait for the chain's time** ([#135](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/135)). A refund leaf is spent once the confirmed chain time passes its locktime, not the clock, so arkd no longer refuses it with `FORFEIT_CLOSURE_LOCKED`.
- **Oracle write recovery accepts any scoring metrics** ([#129](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/129)). After a lost response, recovery refused events not scored on exactly temp_high, temp_low and wind_speed. A competition on other metrics could then not submit its entries and was cancelled.

**API and synth**

- `GET /api/v1/entries?event_id=<id>` filters by competition ([#132](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/132)). The parameter was ignored, and every call returned all of the player's entries.
- Refused ticket and entry requests log at WARN instead of ERROR, and other failures log their causes ([#128](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/128)).
- Synth waits on the ticket when a hold-invoice payment outlasts LND's payment stream, instead of failing the run ([#133](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/133)).

**Player pages** ([#126](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/126), [#130](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/130))

- A **How it works** page (`/help`) with a walkthrough, a how-it-works video, and the explanations the competition pages no longer carry.
- Quieter entry form, leaderboard and picks dialog. Par ranges sit in the pick buttons, and choosing a pick again clears it. The entry form and home page show Price (with a fee breakdown) and Win. There are no On track / Off track labels.
- **One entry per player.** Competitions carry `max_entries_per_player`, default 1. It is checked at ticket reservation and again when an entry is saved.
- New competitions queue by default. A queue's pot shows what its entries so far put in each pool.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the coordinator, ark-swapd and synth from `v2.12.0`. There are no migrations or configuration changes.
2. Deploy the verifier enclave from `v2.12.0` with Keymeld (its `verifierVersion` and the enclave tarball's hash). Until then, escrowed entries' expiry refunds are refused by the old verifier and wait; attested payouts are unaffected.

**Compatibility**

- The escrow payout request's `attestation` is optional; attested requests are byte-identical to v2.11.0's. A v2.11.0 verifier refuses an expiry refund rather than paying it wrongly.
- Rolling back to v2.11.0 is safe until a contract settles on its expiry. After that, v2.11.0 cannot derive an outcome for that competition and retries it until upgraded again.

**Validation**

- `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -D warnings` is clean; the workspace tests pass in CI.
- New tests cover:
  - an expired contract settling on its expiry outcome;
  - the verifier's `settled_outcome` rule;
  - oracle recovery on other metrics;
  - the entries `event_id` filter;
  - a payment stream cut off by the client timeout;
  - the claim leaf's shape and condition witness.
- The claim witness was checked against arkd's own condition evaluation. A live claim against arkd has not yet been run.
