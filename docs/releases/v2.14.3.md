# Coordinator v2.14.3

This release keeps a winner's Lightning payout alive through a routing failure instead of giving up on it, and widens the payout window so routes of more than one hop fit.

**Why**

A payout that LND could not route was treated as final: the coordinator retired the claim, opened a new one about every twenty seconds, and each new claim renewed the enclave's release preparation. The enclave allows sixteen, so after six minutes of `FAILURE_REASON_NO_ROUTE` the entry's Lightning payout was lost and the job retried the exhausted request every five minutes until on-chain settlement. The routing failure itself came from the payout window: the HTLC could lock at most 144 blocks, and every hop of a route adds its own CLTV delta, 80 blocks on a default node, so only a direct channel to the winner ever fit.

**Payouts** ([#169](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/169), [#170](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/170))

- A failure that can pass (no route, timeout, insufficient balance, a routing error, or a reason the coordinator does not know) keeps the claim and the invoice. The payout waits 30 s, then double that up to ten minutes, and the same invoice is sent again while it has not expired and the window is open. Only the payee rejecting the invoice, an expired invoice or a closed window fails a payout.
- An entry gets at most 12 automatic claims; after that it waits for on-chain settlement and the log says so once per tick. The enclave's capacity error fails the job for good with its reason instead of retrying.
- Every send and every failed send is logged with the payout, entry, amount, payee node and CLTV limit; `coordinator_payout_send_failures_total{reason}` counts failed sends.
- The payout HTLC may lock up to 432 blocks, and the default `relative_locktime_block_delta` is 432, so routes of a few hops fit. Competitions already signed keep their delta.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the coordinator from `v2.14.3`. A migration adds `payouts.next_send_at` and `payouts.send_attempts`.
2. Nothing changes for ark-swapd, synth or the verifier; their versions move with the workspace.

**Compatibility**

New competitions are created with a 432-block delta unless the settings say otherwise; the on-chain fallback for an unpaid winner therefore waits longer (twice the delta). Rolling back to v2.14.2 leaves the two new columns unused.

**Validation**

The workspace tests and E2E suite pass in CI. On the test network a payout retried after a refill of the payout channel and succeeded; the first payout that crosses a second hop is the one to watch.
