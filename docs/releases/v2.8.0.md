# Coordinator v2.8.0

This release charges each entry its share of the Bitcoin network fees, and checks every Arkade pool at kickoff before building its contract ([#114](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/114)). A game's chain cost is per game, not a share of the pot, so the service fee alone does not cover it for small pools or when fee rates rise.

**Changes**

- **Network fee per entry.** One entry's share of a 5-player pool's chain cost at the current two-block estimate, with a 50% margin: `ceil((342 + 26 × 5) / 5 × rate × 1.5)` sats, the rate floored at 1 sat/vB. That is 142 sats at 1 sat/vB, 284 at 2 and 425 at 3.
  - The fee is fixed on the ticket's invoice when the ticket is issued. It changes only if the ticket's payment hash rotates.
  - The entry form shows it as its own line, `entry · service · network`, with one line saying what it is. The ticket's own fee replaces the form's estimate once the ticket is issued.
  - The wallet accepts a ticket only if its entry and service fees are the ones the form showed, and its network fee is at most twice the form's estimate.
  - The escrow's fee cap includes it, and a refund returns it with the rest of the escrow.
  - No ticket is issued without a fee estimate. `GET /api/v1/network-fee` returns the current quote.
- **Paused entries.** No ticket is issued while the network fee would be more than 10% of the entry fee. Ticket requests get a 503, and the entry form says entries are paused before any picks are made. Entries already taken are unaffected.
- **Kickoff check.** A pool is checked when it forms, before its oracle event, and every Arkade competition again just before its contract is built. It proceeds only if:
  - what the entries paid beyond the pot covers `(342 + 26 × players)` vbytes at the kickoff rate, plus 0.5% of the pot for paying the winner over Lightning and keeping channels balanced;
  - the kickoff rate is within the fee ceiling in the players' terms;
  - the pool has enough players for the rate: its terms' minimum while the rate is at most 2 sat/vB, and at least 5 above that.

  A pool that fails is cancelled and every entry refunded through its escrow, network fee included. A check that cannot run yet waits and runs again; nothing is built without a passed check. The contract is built at the rate the check passed at. The latest check is returned with the competition as `kickoff_check`.
- **Creation.** A competition of fewer than 5 seats is refused while the fee rate is above 2 sat/vB.
- **Configuration.** `network_fee_settings` and `kickoff_check_settings` hold every weight and threshold. Both are on by default.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the coordinator and synth from `v2.8.0`. NOAA Oracle 2.5.0 and Keymeld v0.7.0 are unchanged.
2. The migration `20261004000000_ticket_network_fee` adds `tickets.network_fee_sats` (0 for existing tickets), `tickets.network_fee_hash` and the `competition_kickoff_checks` table. It only adds, so v2.7.0 keeps running against it during the deploy.

**Compatibility**

- Tickets invoiced before this release keep their price, without a network fee.
- An Arkade competition that took entries before this release is also checked before its contract is built, counting only the service fees its entries paid. At 1 sat/vB, five or more entries at a 3% fee pass; smaller ones, or any after fees rise, are cancelled and refunded.
- Rolling back to v2.7.0 is safe once no ticket issued by v2.8.0 is waiting for payment. v2.7.0 prices a ticket without its network fee, so it would not match such a ticket's swap.

**Known limit**

The verifier signs an escrow into a batch only if the coordinator's fee output is at most that escrow's own fee cap times the number of escrows. Tickets issued at different fee rates have different caps, so a kickoff collects at most the lowest cap times the players, and the rest goes to the Arkade server. The kickoff check counts only what the kickoff collects.

**Validation**

Rust tests cover the fee formula, rounding, the floor and the pause boundary; the kickoff check's exact boundary, fee ceiling, allowance and small-pool minimum; the entry form's price lines and its unavailable and paused states; and the wallet refusing an escrow fee beyond the ticket or an invoice without the fee. Browser unit tests cover the ticket-total checks. A live Arkade run on Mutinynet follows the deploy.
