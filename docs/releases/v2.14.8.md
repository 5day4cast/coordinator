# Coordinator v2.14.8

This release lets the Arkade entries pause see a boarding outage, which v2.14.7 could only see through the coordinator's own batch steps.

**Why**

During a long outage of the Arkade server's boarding, the coordinator ran no batch step of its own for an hour: recoveries were in their backoff, and no ticket reached a kickoff because ark-swapd refused to fund swaps first. ark-swapd saw the server fail every minute, but the coordinator did not, so entries were refused with the wallet's sentence instead of the Arkade one and the gauge stayed at 0.

**ark-swapd**

- `GET /v1/wallet` gains `last_board_failure` (time and message of the last server-side board failure) and `last_board_success_at`.

**Coordinator**

- Once a minute the coordinator reads ark-swapd's wallet view and folds those two into the Arkade health signal, exactly like its own batch steps. A boarding outage now pauses entries with "Entries are paused while the Arkade network recovers; try again in a little while" and sets `coordinator_arkade_unavailable` to 1; the next successful board or batch step lifts it.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy ark-swapd, then the coordinator, from `v2.14.8`. No migrations. Synth is unchanged apart from its version.

**Compatibility**

Rolling back to v2.14.7 is safe.
