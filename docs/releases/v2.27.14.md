# v2.27.14 — Escrow payments no longer wait on an Arkade boarding

ark-swapd's boardings no longer hold the lock that escrow payments take.
While a boarding waited for the Arkade server to take it into a batch, every escrow payment queued behind it, for up to three minutes per attempt and longer in a streak of attempts.
Renewals still hold it, because they can spend the coins a payment would use.
A boarding may now take four batch sessions plus a minute before it is given up; the old bound gave up boardings seconds after their batch had started, and the next attempt began its wait over. Renewals keep the shorter bound.
Electrum lookups that race a new block are tried again, up to three times, instead of failing the coordinator's wallet read.

Synth treats an LND payment lookup that has not answered yet as a payment still in flight, rather than logging a failed lookup every 15 seconds.

This release also carries v2.27.13 for anyone upgrading from v2.27.12.
No configuration or storage changes. The verifier enclave is unchanged; keep the deployed verifier artifact.
Deploy ark-swapd outside a boarding if you can; a slot stopped mid-boarding may wait up to the new boarding bound before it hands over.
