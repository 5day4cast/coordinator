# Coordinator v2.26.2

Opening Satchel now keeps the player logged in to 5day4cast and completes the signed wallet handoff.
The logout listener previously handled every click in the account button container, including Open Satchel.
It could destroy the signer during the handoff and leave the wallet tab blank.
The listener now handles only the Log out button.

A browser regression test uses both production click handlers and a separate wallet origin.
It checks Open Satchel, Pay with Satchel, and explicit logout after asynchronous signing.
