# Coordinator v2.5.3

This release stops settlement from retrying sweeps that would leave dust. The change is in [#100](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/100).

**Changes**

- **Checked sweeps.** Settlement sweeps compute their output with checked arithmetic. A sweep whose fee would leave less than the dust limit is refused instead of broadcast. A fee larger than the output no longer panics.
- **Economy fee for reclaims.** Once their delay has passed, split-reclaims of unpaid winners' outputs pay LND's estimate for confirmation within about a day (144 blocks), instead of the next block.
  - The rate keeps LND's sat/kWU precision.
  - It never goes below LND's floor of 253 sat/kWU.
  - Time-critical transactions keep their fee policy.
- **Outputs that would be dust at any fee rate.** These are outputs that stay dust even at LND's fee rate floor.
  - The output stays on chain and settlement no longer retries it.
  - The entry records `sweep_uneconomic_at` once, and the competition completes.
  - The output remains spendable through the winner's win path and the coordinator's reclaim path.
- **Outputs that are dust only at the current fee rate.** Nothing is recorded, so a later sweep can succeed.
  - A split-reclaim waits, and its competition stays open.
  - A split-close fails the settlement step, which retries it, as after a failed broadcast.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Back up the coordinator database. This release adds migration `20261002000000_entry_sweep_uneconomic`, a nullable `entries.sweep_uneconomic_at` column. It runs at startup.
2. Deploy the coordinator from `v2.5.3`. Synth, the verifier enclaves and the browser WASM are unchanged; synth's version moves with the workspace.

**Compatibility**

- Competitions that retried a rejected reclaim every minute now either sweep at the economy fee or complete with the output recorded as uneconomic.
- Rolling back to v2.5.2 keeps working: older releases ignore the new column.

**Validation**

Rust tests cover:

- the sweep builder at, below and far below the dust limit, including fees that overflow;
- the boundary between outputs that are dust only at the current fee and dust at any fee;
- the economy fee rate's estimate selection, fallback, floor and invalid estimates;
- settlement for the three reclaim cases: waiting, recorded once, and swept at the economy fee.
