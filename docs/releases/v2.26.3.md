# Coordinator v2.26.3

City labels remove more airport descriptors, including Executive, Exec, Metro, Memorial, and Airpark.
For example, Sacramento Exec becomes Sacramento, and Miami Exec Airport becomes Miami.
The same formatting applies to entry forms, saved picks, leaderboard breakdowns, map labels, and city searches.
Full station names and codes remain available in station details.
Names retain their state and geographic qualifiers; the formatter does not infer cities for named fields or counties.
