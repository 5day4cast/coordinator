# v2.27.12 — Operator inventory waits for its load, and the funds trace names the coordinator's close

`GET /api/v1/admin/competitions` waits up to two seconds for a refresh, instead of 250 ms, and still never serves an inventory older than 30 seconds.
Callers that poll minutes apart always need a fresh load, so a load slower than 250 ms was answered 503 at every poll while it went on to succeed.
A 503 now carries `Retry-After`: two seconds while the load is still running, which the next request shares, and 30 seconds after a failed load.
`coordinator_operator_inventory_load_seconds{stage}` times the `competitions`, `refunds`, `write_offs` and `encode` stages and the `total`, and a load slower than the wait logs its stages.
`coordinator_operator_inventory_loads_total{result}`, `coordinator_operator_inventory_loaded_timestamp_seconds` and `coordinator_operator_inventory_unavailable_total{reason}` count loads, record the last good one, and count 503s.

Synth asks again after a 503 whose `Retry-After` is ten seconds or less, up to three attempts.
When the coordinator does not list the competitions that never ran and still hold escrows, Synth keeps its last list on the Stuck money panel with its age and the error, and asks again a minute later instead of five.
`synth_unrefunded_last_listed_timestamp_seconds` and `synth_unrefunded_check_failures_total` record the last good check and the failed ones.

The admin funds trace no longer shows a paid winner's closed share as "Chain recovery · Amount unknown · Verify receipt".
After a successful Lightning payout, the coordinator closing the winner's contract share to its own key reads "Share to coordinator · Closed after LN payout", with the share as its amount.
A share swept after the timelock reads "Share swept to coordinator" and, without a successful payout, points at the owed winners.

No configuration or storage changes. The verifier enclave is unchanged; keep the deployed verifier artifact.
