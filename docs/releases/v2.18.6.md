# Coordinator and Synth v2.18.6

Pool events are now listed on the oracle.
Pools used to copy their queued competition's reference event, including its unlisted flag, so the oracle's event list and status counts left out every pool even though the coordinator's public lists show them.
The reference event, which takes no entries, stays unlisted.
Pools formed before this release keep their existing oracle events.

Players stay logged in across page reloads and tabs.
A login is remembered in the browser for 30 days since the last visit, other tabs follow logins and logouts, and logging out deletes the stored login.

Discover games now shows game setup and the Create competition button above the matching stations and map.

The default competition is one 20-seat pool that pays two places: pools of 10 or more pay 1st 70% and 2nd 30%, and smaller pools pay their winner the pot.
Competitions paying one place may still take up to 25 players.
Two-place pools need the Keymeld release that raises the signing batch cap; until it is deployed, the capacity check refuses queued competitions whose largest pool would pay two places, including the new admin form default.

Settle-only mode lets a coordinator restored from backups finish every money obligation without taking new money.
It is off by default (`[coordinator_settings] settle_only = false`, or `COORDINATOR_SETTLE_ONLY`); while on, new competitions, tickets and entries answer 503 and settlement keeps running.
On every start the coordinator reconciles with the chain and LND: settlement transactions already on chain count as broadcast, failed payouts LND paid are marked paid, and payouts that an unrecorded LND payment could have covered are held for an operator (`coordinator admin payout-holds`).
The health check reports the mode, and `docs/ops/disaster-recovery.md` describes the restore.

Deploy Coordinator to apply these changes.
The `payout_holds` migration adds one table and is roll-forward only.
No configuration changes are required, and Synth, ark-swapd, and the Keymeld gateway and verifier are unchanged.
