# Coordinator v2.14.4

This release makes the how-it-works videos lighter and quicker to start, and gives synth's rebalancer a third leg so winner payouts keep a route on the test network.

**Player UI** ([#172](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/172))

- The two how-it-works recordings carry their music in mono at 40 kb/s under the same picture: the phone cut goes from 955 KB to 691 KB, the desktop cut from 849 KB to 585 KB. The `help-video` recipe encodes that way from now on.
- The video tags preload their metadata, so the index and duration are there before the first tap and play starts at once.

**Synth** ([#173](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/173))

- A payee leg: when the source's share of its channel with the payee (the node behind the players' Lightning Address) falls below `low_percent`, the source creates an invoice and the payee pays it over that channel, up to `target_percent` and capped at `max_sats`. Configured under `rebalance.payee` (`lnd` plus optional `low_percent`, `target_percent`, `max_sats`); without it nothing changes. The dashboard's Liquidity section and rebalance history show the leg.
- Every leg runs on each pass; a failing leg no longer hides the others.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the coordinator and synth from `v2.14.4`. No migrations.
2. For the payee leg: give synth a macaroon on the payee node with `offchain:read`, `offchain:write` and `info:read`, and make sure the source's macaroon (`rebalance.source`) also has `invoices:write`, since the source now creates the invoice the payee pays. Then set `rebalance.payee.lnd`.

**Compatibility**

ark-swapd is unchanged apart from its version. Rolling back to v2.14.3 is safe.

**Validation**

The workspace tests and E2E suite pass in CI. The payee leg has unit tests only; the first pass against live nodes is the one to watch.
