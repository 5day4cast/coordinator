# Coordinator v2.14.0

This release makes a pool's Arkade kickoff fit arkd's session window for pools of 25, stops kickoffs failing when they share a batch with other users' VTXOs, and clears the public-page and synth problems found in review.

**Kickoff signing** ([#142](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/142), [#143](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/143))

- Inside a batch, each Keymeld enclave's share of the contract permits and forfeit signatures is requested side by side; the durable path is unchanged. Each kickoff logs one line with its phase timings: batch finalization, contract signing, forfeit signing, submit.
- The contract signing scope is sent in a compact form (item ids and digests; the verifier derives signers and adaptor points). A verifier older than this release refuses it and the coordinator falls back to the full form. The verifier caches a completed contract's verification for a kickoff's forfeits.
- A 22-player kickoff that took 19–26 s after batch finalization is projected at 5–7 s; the log line confirms it.

**Kickoff without a cosigner key** ([#146](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/146))

- The kickoff intent registers no cosigner key. arkd puts every listed cosigner into a batch's VTXO tree signing, so a kickoff sharing a batch with any VTXO receiver was asked to cosign, refused, failed the round for everyone and got its escrows a conviction each. It has not yet run against a live arkd in a shared batch; watch the kickoff log line and any batch failure right after a kickoff joins.

**Synth and ark-swapd** ([#144](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/144))

- synth follows an entry payment after its LND stream ends, retries GETs after transport errors, accepts a replacement getting another seat, and marks a seat taken by an outside player as Skipped.
- ark-swapd bounds each refund pass to about 5 s so refund claims cannot starve escrow payments, and refuses a swap its wallet cannot fund (503) instead of holding the payer's HTLC.
- Oracle errors from the API return 503.

**Public pages** ([#145](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/145))

- The home page and competitions list read a lean query (about 0.1 MB instead of 1.5 MB of contract blobs) and complete only the rows shown.
- Unlisted single competitions are hidden from the public list; queues and pools stay listed.
- A competition that didn't run says when its refunds open, then "Refunding…", then "Refunded", counting paid entry fees.
- Tooltips no longer clip on phones; badge and muted-text contrast meets 4.5:1; touch targets are 44 px; the WASM wallet loads only on log-in or pay.
- End states read "Finished" or "Didn't run"; "service fee" everywhere.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the verifier enclave from `v2.14.0` with Keymeld first (`verifierVersion` and the enclave tarball's hash). It accepts both signing-scope forms.
2. Deploy the coordinator, ark-swapd and synth from `v2.14.0`. There are no migrations or configuration changes.

**Compatibility**

A `v2.14.0` coordinator against the `v2.13.0` verifier keeps working: the verifier refuses the compact scope while decoding it, before any state changes, and the coordinator re-sends the full form. Rolling back to v2.13.2 is safe.

**Validation**

The workspace tests and E2E suite pass in CI. New tests cover the per-enclave split (order, failure of one share, the durable path unchanged), the compact scope against the full one, the verify-once cache, the cosigner-free kickoff in a batch with a VTXO tree, the lean list against the full read, and the synth and ark-swapd changes.
