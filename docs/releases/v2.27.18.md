# v2.27.18 — Faster competition pages, and a full competition says it is full

Public competition pages no longer decode the competition's signed contract, which none of them shows.
Decoding it rebuilt every outcome's transactions: for a 20-entry pool paying two places that was most of the time its leaderboard took to load. Leaderboards, entry forms, forecasts and the competition lists now read only what they display. Competitions settled by their expiry transaction are still read in full, since telling whether the pot went back by expiry needs the contract.

A visitor's entry form now says "This competition is full." when every seat is paid for or held for an unpaid invoice, as the competition's badge already did, instead of "Entries have closed."
Synth counts held seats the way the coordinator does, and no longer records a competition that filled as a failed visitor check. Deploy Synth from this release together with the coordinator.

No configuration or schema changes are required. The verifier enclave is unchanged; keep the deployed verifier artifact.
