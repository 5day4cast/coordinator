# Coordinator v2.8.1

This release prices each entry's network fee at the rate its pool's contract is built at ([#116](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/116)).

**Fix**

Contracts are built at the fee estimate rounded up to a whole sat/vB, and the kickoff check costs a pool at that rate. v2.8.0 priced tickets at the exact estimate. At the lowest estimate, 1.012 sat/vB, a ticket was priced at 1.012 but its pool was checked at 2. Two- and three-player pools therefore always failed their kickoff check and were refunded, and a five-player pool failed once the estimate rose past 2 sat/vB before kickoff.

Tickets are now priced at the rounded-up rate, so the check compares like with like:

- The network fee is 284 sats at the lowest estimate, up from 144.
- For a 5,000 sat entry, entries pause while the rate is above 3 sat/vB, where v2.8.0 paused above about 4.2.

**Required upgrade steps**

Deploy the coordinator and synth from `v2.8.1`. There are no migrations. Tickets issued by v2.8.0 keep the network fee fixed on their invoices.

**Compatibility**

Rolling back to v2.8.0 is safe. It prices new tickets lower again, and tickets already issued keep their fees.

**Validation**

Rust tests cover pricing at the rounded-up rate, small pools priced at the lowest estimate passing their check, and a five-player pool passing when the estimate more than doubles before kickoff.
