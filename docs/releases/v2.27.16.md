# v2.27.16 — Synth's weather picker steps around storms

Synth's picker leads with the station that has the most weather, which during a landfalling tropical storm was the storm itself; six queued pools in a row then expired because the storm's stations stopped reporting.
The picker now leaves out every candidate whose forecast reaches at least 45 mph of wind with at least 2.0 in of rain over the window, and every candidate within 300 km of one, for the leader and the other picks alike.
Stations the lane will not pick still count as storm sources, so a storm stays visible after its own stations go quiet. Snow and ice do not count; the picker still looks for them.
Scores are scaled after storms are removed, so a storm's wind no longer flattens every other station's wind component.
Against the recorded picks, the guard leaves out a pick in all six storm pools and in none of the 28 pools that settled.

`picker.storm` sets `enabled`, `wind_mph`, `rain_in` and `radius_km`; a lane without the table gets these defaults. Saved picks list the stations left out and the strongest storm forecasts, and picks saved by earlier versions still load.
Only Synth changes. If `picker.storm` is added to a Synth config, roll it back together with the binary: earlier versions refuse unknown keys.
