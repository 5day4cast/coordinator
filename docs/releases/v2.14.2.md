# Coordinator v2.14.2

This release stops escrow VTXOs from expiring on the Arkade server before their refund can spend them, recovers the ones that already did, keeps ark-swapd's coins fresh, and makes ticket requests fast again.

**Why**

Arkade VTXOs live seven days from the batch they descend from, and an offchain payment inherits its source coin's expiry. ark-swapd paid escrows from its oldest coins, so escrows could be born with a day of life left; a didn't-fill refund that opened a day after the start then found its VTXO swept (`VTXO_RECOVERABLE`) and minted a new swap every hour, forever. The funding check added in v2.14.1 also listed every VTXO the wallet ever held on each swap, which made ticket requests take 5–30 s.

**ark-swapd** ([#166](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/166))

- A renewal step settles recoverable coins and any coin with under `renew_margin_days` (3) of life, regardless of boarding coins.
- Escrows are paid only from coins with at least `pay_margin_days` (2) left, longest-lived first; the funding refusal names what is awaiting renewal.
- Wallet reads list only unspent VTXOs, in parallel, cached for 10 s; `GET /v1/wallet` gains `payable_sat`, `expiring_sat` and `earliest_expiry`.

**Escrow refunds** ([#167](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/167))

- The refund locktime is the start plus `refund_after_start_secs` (default 45 minutes, was a day), capped at the contract's expiry. When a ticket's payment is confirmed, the escrow VTXO's expiry is checked against the refund time plus `escrow_expiry_margin_secs`; a coin that would expire first does not pay the ticket.
- A refund Arkade refuses as expired is held with a note, keeps its swap, and is not minted again. An escrow listed as swept is recovered in a batch: a refund intent through the refund leaf, signed by the player through Keymeld under the existing refund permission (new `RefundIntent` purpose) and by the coordinator, paying the swap as the batch's VTXO; then the player is paid as usual. Failed recoveries retry every ten minutes.
- The verifier now signs an offchain refund only under the refund permission, even for a bound pool, so the invoice, fee and deadline checks always run.

**Synth** ([#165](https://github.com/5day4cast/coordinator/pull/165))

- Competitions are created with the 3% service fee the site charges, not 10%.

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the verifier enclave from `v2.14.2` with Keymeld first (`verifierVersion` and the enclave tarball's hash): recoveries need the new purpose.
2. Deploy ark-swapd, the coordinator and synth from `v2.14.2`. No migrations. New optional settings: ark-swapd `renew_margin_days`, `pay_margin_days`; coordinator `refund_after_start_secs`, `escrow_expiry_margin_secs`.

**Compatibility**

Existing escrows keep the refund locktime in their tap tree. Rolling back to v2.14.1 is safe; held refunds go back to the hourly remint loop.

**Validation**

The workspace tests and E2E suite pass in CI, including a scripted recovery batch with real MuSig2 nonces and signatures and an in-process Keymeld signing it. Nothing has run against a live arkd yet: watch the first recovery and the first renewal in the logs.
