# Coordinator v2.4.1

This release fixes competitions that stopped with their pot still in the contract, gives each competition one status across the sites, and makes the log-in and sign-up dialogs readable in light mode.

**Compatible components**

| Component | Version |
| --- | --- |
| Keymeld gateway | `v0.5.0` or later |
| Keymeld SDK, core and enclave crates | `v0.6.0` |
| Coordinator verifier enclave and LNURL relay | `v2.4.0` or `v2.4.1` |
| ark-swapd | `v2.4.1` |
| synth | `v2.4.1` |
| NOAA Oracle API | `v2.3.0` or later |
| dlctix | crates.io `0.1.0` |

**Changes**

- The split-reclaim waits for the split transaction to confirm and then for its reclaim delay. Before, it was sent a minute after the split, was rejected, and failed the competition with its pot on-chain. (#88)
- After funding confirms, errors while checking the attestation or broadcasting the outcome, delta or reclaim transactions no longer fail the competition. It retries and keeps its last five errors. (#88)
- Competitions that an earlier version failed or cancelled while their contract held the pot are picked up again and settle from where they stopped. Ones that never had a confirmed contract stay cancelled. (#88)
- A competition whose contract was funded never shows as "Cancelled". One settled on the refund outcome reads "Refunded", and one that didn't fill shows whether its refunds are pending or done. Competitions that didn't run show no ranks or scores. (#88)
- The log-in, sign-up and password dialogs follow the page theme. Labels, help text and errors read at 4.5:1 or better. (#88)
- Long status badges wrap inside their column. The phone menu closes when a dialog opens from it. "Log in" is used everywhere. (#88)
- Refund failures log their cause instead of "internal error". (#88)
- synth: the hop and stuck-money tables fit the page. A run with stuck money is badged stuck, not passed. Sats are grouped by thousands. A stuck run's reason stays current. (#87, #88)

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Back up the coordinator database. This release adds no migration.
2. Deploy ark-swapd and synth from this release together with the coordinator. No Keymeld or enclave change is needed.
3. After deploying, look for "Resuming settlement of competition …" in the coordinator log, once for each competition it picks up again.

**Limits**

- When a split output is reclaimed, the coordinator logs that the unpaid winner is still owed. It does not pay that winner over Lightning. An operator has to pay them out of band, and nothing records that payment.
- Escrows for tickets paid before v2.4.0 and never entered have no Keymeld registration, so their refunds need an operator (`docs/ops/stuck-escrow-check.md`).
- The log-in WASM is still 6.4 MB (3.2 MB compressed).
