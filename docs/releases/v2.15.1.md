# Coordinator v2.15.1

This release makes the synthetic traffic keep a competition open for real visitors, choose its cities by the weather, and adds operator tools for stress tests and quick competitions.

**Coordinator**

- The public competition response carries `min_players_now` while a competition collects entries: the terms' minimum raised by the kickoff check at the current fee estimate, so a client knows how many players a pool still needs (#201).
- CI allows clippy's `double_must_use` on `#[async_trait]` traits under Rust 1.98 and synth's operator nonce uses `rand::rng` (#204); two entry-flow unit tests announce payment events for the right ticket (#205).

**Synth**

- Lane fill mode `backfill`: a competition is created at the pool cap and stays open until its entry deadline; `early_players` enter spread over the window and, `backfill_before_close_secs` before entries close, synth enters only as many more players as the fee-based minimum plus `backfill_margin` still needs, counting everyone's paid entries. Money checks treat strangers' entries, pots and wins as normal. `scheduler.keep_open` starts the named lane's next run early when no listed competition is collecting entries with `min_minutes_left` to go. Gauges `synth_open_competitions`, `synth_open_competition_minutes_left`; counters `synth_keep_open_starts_total`, `synth_backfill_players_total` (#201).
- Scenario `stress_full_pool` enters players in a burst at the pool cap with bounded concurrency, recording every refusal and per-step latency; a dashboard form and `synth run manual-competition` create a competition on demand with an optional number of synth players (#202).
- Lane `picker.mode = "weather"`: cities are drawn from the oracle's eligible stations (falling back to the lane's list), scored on the forecast's temperature swing, wind, precipitation and extremes, led by a recognisable airport and clustered within `cluster_km`; the pick and its reasons are recorded on the run (#203).

**Required upgrade steps**

1. Deploy the coordinator and synth from `v2.15.1`. No migrations. New optional synth settings named above; the open lane's settings change separately.
2. The weather picker's `candidates = "eligible"` needs oracle 2.6.8 or later for `/stations/eligible`; without it the lane's fixed list is used.

**Compatibility**

Rolling back to v2.15.0 is safe once the new synth settings are removed.
