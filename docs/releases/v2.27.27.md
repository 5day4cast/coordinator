# v2.27.27 — Pay Arkade's intent fee

Arkade servers publish fee programs in `/v1/info` and refuse any intent that pays less than they charge (`INTENT_INSUFFICIENT_FEE`). A server may charge for inputs by their age: for example, 0.2% of every VTXO input younger than a day. Two of our intents paid less than that:

- **Pool kickoffs.** The coordinator sent the escrows' surplus beyond the pool to its own fee output, up to the escrows' fee cap, and left the server only the remainder. Every escrow is younger than a day at kickoff, so the server refused the intent until the kickoff deadline passed and the competition was cancelled. The kickoff now reads the server's fee programs fresh, sets the server's intent fee aside from the surplus, and takes its own fee from what remains. Players' stakes and the pool are unchanged.
- **Wallet renewals and boards (ark-swapd).** The rust-sdk fork's `settle` sent every input's full amount to its own output and paid no intent fee. It now prices each input with the server's fee programs, using each VTXO's birth, expiry and type, and pays the inputs' and the output's fees from the settled output.

Fees round up per input, so the total is never below the server's.

Tests cover the fee for twenty young 5,382-sat escrows (220 sats against the 216 a server asked), escrows older than a day, recoverable inputs, and servers that publish no fee programs.
